Money questions

Asked and answered

Is there a contract?

No. The $200 is one time. The $49 a month is month to month and you can cancel with thirty days' notice, for any reason.

What does the $49 a month cover?

Hosting, SSL, daily backups, security updates, uptime monitoring, and up to an hour of text and photo changes each week. Text them to us and they're done that week.

What if I need more than an hour of changes?

Most weeks you won't come close — an hour covers a lot of text and photo edits, and we're not counting minutes. If something bigger comes up, like a new page or a new service to add, we'll tell you what it costs before we start and you can say no.

Are there hidden costs?

Domain registration runs about $15–20 a year, billed to you directly by the registrar — deliberately, so ownership stays in your name. Nothing else.

What happens if I cancel?

We export your site, transfer the domain to your account, and help you move to another host. You keep the design, the copy, and the files.

Why is this so much cheaper than other agencies?

Because we build one kind of thing, over and over, for one kind of business. We're not doing discovery workshops or brand strategy decks. You get a good website that does its job, fast.

Ready when you are.

Tell us what you do and we'll confirm the price on the first call. No obligation, and no pitch if it's not a fit.
