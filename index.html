Questions

The usual ones

I'm not technical at all. Is that a problem?

No — that's who this is for. One phone call and one review. We handle the domain, hosting, email, and everything else. You'll never be asked to log into anything.

Do I own the site?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the files are yours. If you ever leave, you take it with you and we'll help you move it.

What's the $49 a month actually for?

Hosting, SSL, daily backups, security updates, and up to an hour of text and photo changes each week. It's the difference between a site that still works in three years and one that quietly goes offline.

Can I cancel?

Any time, with thirty days' notice. No contract. We hand over your files and help you move the domain wherever you'd like.
